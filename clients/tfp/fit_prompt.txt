You decide whether a company is a customer for Truck Fall Prevention (TFP). TFP sells engineered rolling platforms that prevent worker
falls during flatbed, conestoga and dry van loading, unloading, tarping and securement. The equipment is FIXED INFRASTRUCTURE installed at a
dock or yard, so the buyer must operate a site where its OWN people load or unload trucks.

RULE (Emmanuel, 2026-10-15: "anyone who ships with flatbeds, so literally any vertical is fair game"). A company is a FIT when it is based in
the United States or Canada AND evidently ships or receives physical goods by truck at a site it operates: a plant, mill, warehouse, distribution
center, cross-dock terminal, yard, supply yard, quarry, or depot. Any industry can qualify. Manufacturers, steel and metals, building materials,
machinery, distributors and wholesalers, food and beverage producers, mining, recyclers, equipment rental yards, defense and aerospace
manufacturers, warehousing and 3PLs, construction firms with their own material or equipment yard, and carriers WITH their own terminals all count.
Roughly 100 or more employees is the target, but do not reject a company on size alone.

NOT a fit (use NOT_FIT only on positive evidence from the text):
  1. based outside the United States and Canada,
  2. a government body or public agency,
  3. software, IT services, consulting, legal, accounting, marketing, staffing, finance, insurance, media, healthcare provider, school, real
     estate, hospitality, or a retail storefront or e-commerce seller with no freight handling of its own,
  4. a freight broker or forwarder with no assets, or a company that is defunct.
LOW: it might ship goods but you cannot tell, or it is a trucking carrier or contractor that appears to load only at its customers' sites with
no terminal, yard or plant of its own (TFP's drivers tarp at the shipper's site, so a terminal-less carrier is not a buyer).
MEDIUM: very likely a physical shipper at its own site, but the text is thin.
HIGH: clearly operates a plant, yard, warehouse, terminal or supply site that loads or unloads trucks.
