Lumion (lumion.ai) is an enrollment platform built for private trade schools and career colleges.
Sender voice: Drew Jones, Lumion (andrew.jones@lumion.ai). Casual founder voice. Buyers are "light blue
collar": they want more seats, faster enrollment and faster tuition, in plain words with no software
vocabulary.

Problem: four leaks between click and first class. Lead capture, follow-up speed (especially text
messages), application to enrollment drop-off, and students who cannot pay upfront.

Products and the one line each may use in copy (one mechanism line per email, never all five):
- Lead capture and dynamic enrollment forms: "Lumion catches every visitor who shows interest on your
  site and starts the conversation by text in minutes."
- Text follow-up and admissions CRM: "Lumion texts every inquiry within minutes and keeps following up,
  so students hear from you before anyone else."
- Voice AI phone agent: "Lumion answers every call in your school's voice, day or night, and books a real
  callback before they hang up."
- Mobile application (VERIFY-1, wording stays as in the reference drafts): "Lumion turns your application
  into a mobile form students can finish from their phone in one sitting."
- Payments: "Lumion puts payment plans directly into your admissions flow so students see options
  instantly and say yes on the spot."

Proof: never numbers, and never a school name in an email (Emmanuel, 2026-10-09). Customers for reference only: Hoosier Trade School, Arclabs Welding,
Savant Aesthetics Institute, CDL Training of Tampa, Dasmesh Truck Driving School, Berry Best Phlebotomy,
Tripleten, Coding Temple, Iron Circle, Train For The Crane, RiseAero, FlexAir. The vertical to school map
is school-map.json. Unapproved or missing verticals use the fallback question.

CTA, exactly one per email: "Want me to send over what the first 30 days looked like for a similar {school type}?" (for example a similar healthcare
school or a similar bootcamp). Never name a school.
Fallback: "Want me to send over what the first 30 days looked like for a school doing your volume?"

Never: pricing (deflect to a call), case study numbers, competitor names, loss framing such as "stop
losing", dash characters in the email body, anything marked VERIFY beyond the reference drafts' wording.

The three angles under test, one per campaign, each carrying every vertical:
1. pdf-application: the school's enrollment application is a PDF students print and bring in.
2. after-5pm: the office closes at 5, so after-hours callers hit voicemail. Voice AI answers and books a callback.
3. next-start-date: a published start date approaches while older inquiries sit unworked.
